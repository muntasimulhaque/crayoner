Crayoner: Privacy Policy

Crayoner does not collect, share, or transmit any data. Ever.

Crayoner is a paid, offline coloring book for young children. It has no advertising, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, and no internet permission at all. The app cannot connect to the network, because the Android system gives it no way to.

What stays on your device

  • The sound on/off switch.
  • Which pictures have been finished, so the shelf can show a star beside them.
  • The colors of the one page currently being colored, so a phone call or a restart does not lose them.

These preferences never leave your device, and only one page in progress is kept at a time. Uninstalling the app removes them permanently.

What we never do

  • We never collect personal data of any kind, from anyone.
  • We never show ads or promote other products.
  • We never send anything over the internet.
  • We never ask a child (or a parent) for a name, an email, an age, or a location.

Children

Crayoner is designed for young children and complies with Google Play's Families policy. Because it collects nothing, transmits nothing, and asks for no permission at all, there is nothing about a child for it to store anywhere but on the family's own device.

Permissions

Crayoner declares no Android permissions. Not internet, not storage, not anything. You can confirm this in the app's listing on Google Play, or in the source code on GitHub.

Open source

Crayoner is open source under the MIT license. The complete source code is at github.com/muntasimulhaque/crayoner, so anyone can read exactly what the app does.
