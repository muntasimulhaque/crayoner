Crayoner

A simple, beautiful coloring book for children aged 3 to 5. Sixteen pictures, one box of crayons, and a finished picture above every page to copy. Paid once, no ads, no trackers, no accounts, and no permissions at all.

Privacy policy
muntasimulhaque.github.io/crayoner/privacy.html

Crayoner collects nothing and sends nothing. The full policy, in plain language, is one page long.

The app

  • Sixteen pictures: a sailboat, a tree, a balloon, an ice cream, a mushroom, a kite, flowers, a rainbow, a cupcake, a house, a car, an umbrella, a rocket, a train, a lighthouse and a castle.
  • One crayon and one rubber on the tray, with all thirty two colors of a real box a press away. The same crayon and the same rubber every page every time.
  • No score, no timer, no fail state, and no picture ever locked.
